# Revenue Streams and Risk

Candidate lanes:
- Service revenue: Telstar consulting, integrations and maintenance. Usually faster to test than software, but less passive.
- Software revenue: licensed workflow tools, templates, SaaS. Recurring potential plus support and acquisition burden.
- Digital assets: practical guides, worksheets, eBooks. Low marginal cost but significant distribution and quality burden.
- Creative/IP: games and consumer products. Longer, uncertain timelines.

Measure separately: employment earnings, business receipts, refunds, payment processing, ads, hosting, model/API charges, supplies and fulfillment. Never label gross sales 'profit' or count speculative future revenue as recurring income.

Avoid building many simultaneous products without paying users; prioritize by real signals, capital exposure and time to earliest test. See [[07-Business/Portfolio]] and [[09-Metrics/Business Scorecard]].
