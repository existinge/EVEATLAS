# Telstar / Eve Studio — services first

Positioning: **Eve is the business; Telstar is the product.** Keep consulting/automation services and the longer-term product as distinct lanes.

## Initial buyers
Small organizations and professionals with painful repetitive processes: intake, reminders, operations handoffs, document coordination, scheduling, reporting and knowledge retrieval. Confirm scope and compliance before handling regulated data.

## Test offer
A narrow, easily explainable paid automation/operational cleanup service with clear deliverables, boundaries, timeline and support terms. Do not claim customers, traction, integrations or deployed capabilities without evidence.

## Sales validation
Research workflows, interview prospects with permission, demonstrate a working scoped prototype, quote a price grounded in costs and value; solicit a paid pilot only after the solution is safe and feasible.

## Operational cautions
Avoid processing medical records or other regulated personal data without appropriate infrastructure and agreements. Don't access employer/client proprietary code as a commercial product. Approve integrations and API budgets explicitly.
