# Business Portfolio

**Mission:** several separate, sustainable income sources, prioritized by measured net contribution, repeatability, ownership, defensibility and time cost.

| Venture | Model | Evidence status | Next gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Telstar / Eve Studio | AI systems consulting and SMB automation | positioning and website development; paid traction not verified | select a narrow offer, run customer discovery |
| Shift Tally | workflow software/product candidate | prototype exists; external buyers unverified | interview potential customers and assess permission/IP |
| Opportunity Engine | internal research infrastructure; possible later product | workflow defined, demand validation pending | perform one sourced pilot |
| Digital publishing | guides, templates, eBooks | speculative distribution opportunity | choose problem and validate before authoring |
| Shopper / outreach agents | services or tools | conceptual; risks and platform permissions | test one lawful measurable use case |
| Chatbot marketplace | reusable automation templates | conceptual | identify paid buyer and support burden |
| Project Indigo | game/IP | creative development; not verified commercial | plan playable milestone before monetization |
| Desk pet | consumer hardware/software | R&D concept; higher costs | validate demand/cost before manufacturing |

**Evidence labels:** idea, researched, customer-validated, revenue-verified, scalable. Never promote status without documentation.

## Portfolio rule
One core income experiment and at most one secondary lightweight test at a time. Limit API/spend exposure and preserve employment stability. Count net profit only after costs. Distinguish public vs private evidence.
