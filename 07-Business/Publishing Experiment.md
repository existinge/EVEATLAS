# Publishing Experiment — niche guides and eBooks

Source concept: Reddit discussions → specific painful questions → tailored practical guide → publish/distribute → measure. **A promotional X thread is not income evidence.**

## Discovery
Find repeatable, answerable problems with specific audiences and distinguishability from plentiful free resources. Independently verify a buying audience through price/comparable sales where possible and small ethical tests.

## Production
Write an accurate original outline; draft chapter by chapter; verify facts, add unique examples, exercises and tested resources; proofread; create an appropriate cover; review legality, intellectual property and accessibility.

Never equate automated drafting with publication readiness. No guaranteed rankings or income from 20–30 books. Treat AI detectors' 'human scores' as unreliable; focus on genuine quality and required disclosure.

## Amazon KDP
Check current platform AI-generated/AI-assisted content disclosure rules, metadata requirements, categories, price/royalties, edition constraints and review/advertising terms before publishing. Do not buy/incentivize/manipulate reviews. A free launch does not guarantee legitimate reviews.

## Paid promotion
No `$5/day` auto-ad campaigns without explicit human approval and a stop-loss budget. Measure net contribution after royalties, ads, refunds, taxes and time.
