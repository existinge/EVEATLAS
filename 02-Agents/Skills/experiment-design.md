# Skill: experiment-design

Given a documented opportunity, create the cheapest ethical test that could falsify demand: interview, pre-sales interest test where permitted, manual concierge prototype or small paid pilot.

Pre-register success/failure threshold, data collection, duration, maximum approved cost and stop conditions. Avoid moving the goalposts after results. No external posting, messaging or spend without approval.

Deliver a filled [[06-Templates/Experiment Template]] and a recommendation: proceed, change, or stop.
