# ATLAS Profile — BL Taste & Discovery
Status: contextual reference, not a watchlist or source of verified title metadata.

## Purpose and loading
Load for Asian live-action M/M discovery, filtering, media recommendations, and cross-domain projects involving romantic storytelling or Asian media. Do not apply this profile as an exclusion filter to unrelated work.

## Positive signals
- Central, reciprocal male/male romantic development with an earned on-screen payoff, not bromance or a side-couple-only story.
- Strongest signal: **intentional, repeated, playful pursuit** — teasing, pestering, flirting, bickering, choosing to spend time together, or a confident pursuer gently flustering the other person while respecting boundaries.
- Lively, warm rom-com energy; convincing early chemistry; natural acting; satisfying progression rather than misunderstandings substituting for romance.
- Best friends-to-lovers, school/university romance, Japanese and Taiwanese BL, and thoughtfully realized age-gap dynamics can be positives.
- Countries: JP/KR/TW/TH are frequent priorities, but investigate internationally and include obscure, local, indie, archival and regional productions.
- Prefer fuller stories over extremely short verticals; shorter work can still qualify if the relationship is developed.

## Specialist research lane (not a universal filter)
A feminine-presenting **male** love interest or an initial mistaken-for-a-girl attraction dynamic, with genuine, consensual central M/M romance. This is a separate research lane. Do not misclassify trans women as men and do not gate general BL discovery on this trope.

## Cautions and hard boundaries
- Exclude AI-generated dramas, coercion or abuse romanticized as the couple's appeal, bromance-only works and mistaken side-couple recommendations.
- Strongly deprioritize mafia/crime focus, dark supernatural/ghost romance, exhausting melodrama, love triangles, generic fake dating or forced proximity without chemistry, and relationship-established-from-start stories.
- Forced proximity, fake dating, rivals/enemies and darker stories are **not automatically banned**; evaluate pursuit, chemistry, health and payoff individually.
- Avoid repetitive, awkward or overly theatrical dialogue, prolonged stalls, and disappointing finale reversals.
- Do not declare a title watched, skipped, or rated based on inference.

## Critical state and authority
- The canonical BL tracker/workbook, latest reconciled reaction history and explicit user corrections govern statuses, aliases, scores and exclusions.
- A 'Maybe' remains backlog, not Watched; Watching remains in progress. Latest status corrections win.
