## Rules for research agents

1. Read the canonical workbook and `WATCH` before recommending anything.
2. Read `Canonical Audit Log`, `Viewing Reconciliation 2026`, `Oct 2026 Overrides`, `Status Review Queue`, and `Manual Overrides`.
3. Normalize original-language and alternate names. An absent exact English title is not enough to declare a discovery.
4. Never confuse `No / Skip` with `Completed`, or `Not Tonight` with a permanent rejection.
5. Treat latest explicit user corrections as higher priority than old generated estimates.
6. Keep ongoing research candidate files under `BL_ENGINE_RESEARCH_YYYY-MM-DD_<topic>.xlsx`; do not mutate canonical scores without an explicit correction.
7. Refer to [the full swarm protocol](./research/BL_DISCOVERY_SWARM_MASTER.md).
