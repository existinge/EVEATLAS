## Definitions
- Gross receipts: amounts actually received from paying customers.
- Net contribution: gross receipts minus refunds, payment fees, direct product/API/hosting/ads costs (before all taxes and allocated overhead).
- Conversion: paid customers / qualified leads (only when denominator known).
- Repeat purchases and retention: measured cohorts, not projections.
- Hourly return: net contribution divided by project hours when measured.

Keep unknowns blank; no illustrative number should look like real revenue. See [[07-Business/Revenue Streams]].
