# Validation Playbook

For every opportunity, preserve publicly accessible permalinks, observation dates, user situation, direct need, available solutions, contradiction, payment signals and confidence. Avoid collecting personal data that isn't needed.

## Signal hierarchy
1. Actual attributable paid transactions for this solution (strongest).
2. Credible deposits, letters of intent or accepted paid pilot terms (still context-dependent).
3. Interviews with clear budget authority and repeated operational costs.
4. Competitor pricing/reviews and revealed buying behavior.
5. Surveys, search interest, complaints, votes and likes (weak alone).

Use independently sourced repetitions rather than one viral thread. Obtain explicit approval for outreach, paid ads and publication.

## Stage gates
Idea → evidence dossier → falsifiable experiment → measured results → minimal offer → launch → repeatable distribution → potential expansion.
